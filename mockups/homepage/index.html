Areas of specialty

How I can help

Every body, every age, and every relationship with food is different. Care is individualized, collaborative, and free of food rules, whether we’re working on a toddler’s plate or an adult’s recovery.

Pediatrics

Picky eating, ARFID, food allergies and intolerances, tube feeding support, and more, with parents as partners.

Eating Disorder Recovery

Eating disorders are serious but treatable. Together we’ll work toward intuitive eating and healing your relationship with food and body.

Disordered Eating & Chronic Dieting

Step off the diet cycle, rebuild trust with hunger and fullness, and eat without the rules.

Body Image Healing

Meet your dietitian

Hey there! I’m Christine

I’m a Registered Dietitian specializing in eating disorders and pediatric nutrition in Dubuque and throughout Iowa, Illinois, and Wisconsin. I work with children, teens, and adults navigating everything from selective eating and nutrition concerns to ARFID, eating disorders, and challenges with food and body image.

I’ve practiced for over 15 years in Eastern Iowa, and I’m a mom to three, so I know mealtime stress firsthand. Nutrition doesn’t have to look perfect. My kids eat mac and cheese and ice cream too!

My approach

All foods fit. Every body deserves care.

Adequate nourishment, flexibility with food, body respect, and a sustainable relationship with eating, without shame or food rules.

  • HAES-informed

    Health is supported at every size. Weight isn’t the measure of progress or the goal of care.

  • Weight-inclusive

    Your body is welcome here as it is today. We focus on behaviors, wellbeing, and trust with food.

  • Evidence-based

    Grounded in current research on eating disorders, pediatric feeding, and nutrition science.
