My approach

All foods fit. Every body deserves care.

Adequate nourishment, flexibility with food, body respect, and a sustainable relationship with eating, without shame or food rules.

  • HAES-informed

    Health is supported at every size. Weight isn’t the measure of progress or the goal of care.

  • Weight-inclusive

    Your body is welcome here as it is today. We focus on behaviors, wellbeing, and trust with food.

  • Evidence-based

    Grounded in current research on eating disorders, pediatric feeding, and nutrition science.

Meet your dietitian

Hey there! I’m Christine

Nutrition doesn’t have to look perfect. My kids eat mac and cheese and ice cream too! It’s all about finding the right balance for your family.

I’m a Registered Dietitian specializing in eating disorders and pediatric nutrition, with over 15 years of practice in Eastern Iowa and a mom to three. I work with children, teens, and adults navigating everything from selective eating and ARFID to eating disorders and challenges with body image.

What to expect

Starting is often the hardest part. Here’s exactly what happens after you reach out.

  1. 120 minutes · Free

    Discovery call

    Ask questions, share your goals, and talk through what you hope to get from our work together.

  2. 260–90 minutes

    Initial assessment

    An in-depth look at your health history, relationship with food, and nutritional needs, and the next steps toward your goals.

  3. 330–40 minutes

    Follow-up sessions

    Build skills and strategies, review progress, answer questions, and set new goals at every appointment.
